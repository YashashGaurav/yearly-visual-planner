What this app does

Yearly Visual Planner is a browser-based year-view calendar for Google Calendar. When you sign in, the app reads your calendar events and displays them in a grid entirely within your browser. Nothing is transmitted to any server operated by the developer.

Google account permissions requested

The app requests two OAuth 2.0 scopes:

Scope What it's used for
calendar.readonly Read-only access to your calendar events. Events are fetched from Google's API and rendered in the year-view grid directly in your browser. The app never modifies your calendars and never stores event data outside your browser session.
drive.appdata Read/write access to a single app-private file (settings002.json) in your own Google Drive appDataFolder. This is used to save your display preferences (title, month count, layout options) so they persist across sessions and devices. The app cannot read, list, or modify any other file in your Drive.

Data storage

All processing happens in your browser. The developer operates no servers, databases, or analytics. Specifically:

  • Google Drive — your settings file (settings002.json) is stored in your own Drive appDataFolder, accessible only to this app and to you.
  • Browser localStorage — two keys are stored locally on your device:
    • vp-gridviewinfo — current view mode (column/list, expand/collapse, dark mode)
    • vp-caltoginfo — per-calendar visibility toggles
  • OAuth access token — the Google OAuth token is cached in localStorage so you are not re-prompted on reload. It is short-lived and scoped only to the permissions above. It is never sent to any third party.

No calendar event data, personal information, or usage data is ever transmitted to the developer or any third party.

Data sharing

The app does not share any data with third parties. There is no advertising, analytics, or telemetry.

Data retention and deletion

Your settings file in Google Drive persists until you delete it. You can delete it by revoking access to this app at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which also immediately revokes the OAuth token and prevents the app from accessing your account. localStorage data is cleared when you clear your browser's site data.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy
